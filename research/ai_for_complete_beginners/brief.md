## Market Analysis
**Demand:** HIGH  
**Why now:** Generative AI tools like ChatGPT, Copilot, and Gemini have made AI accessible to non-technical professionals for the first time, and employers increasingly expect every role to demonstrate basic AI literacy. Millions of knowledge workers need a confident foundation in AI concepts before they can adopt these tools effectively.  
**Target learner:** Non-technical professionals aged 28-55 — project managers, marketers, HR specialists, small business owners, and career changers — who hear about AI daily but feel overwhelmed, unsure what is hype versus real, and worried about falling behind.  
**Learner goal:** Confidently explain core AI concepts, distinguish between AI types, evaluate which everyday AI tools solve which problems, and complete a simple hands-on AI workflow using a free tool — all without writing a single line of code.  
**Gap this fills:** Most beginner AI courses either dive into Python and math too quickly (losing the true beginner) or stay entirely conceptual with no hands-on practice. This course fills the gap by keeping theory jargon-free AND giving learners real, guided exercises with free tools so they finish with practical experience, not just vocabulary.  
**Best platform:** LinkedIn Learning and Udemy are ideal because learners on these platforms expect structured progression with certificates they can share with employers. The topic maps perfectly to professional upskilling budgets and corporate learning libraries, where AI literacy is now a top-requested category.
